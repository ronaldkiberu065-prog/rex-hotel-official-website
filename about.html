Our Story

Rex Restaurant & Takeaway is dedicated to serving customers in Mukono with enjoyable meals and friendly service. We aim to create a place where individuals, families, friends and colleagues can enjoy good food together.

Our restaurant focuses on a variety of meals, including local dishes, snacks, refreshments and other customer favourites.

Whether customers choose to dine in or take their food away, we strive to make their experience convenient and satisfying.

We believe that a successful restaurant is built on quality, trust, cleanliness and good relationships with customers.

Quality and Hygiene

We understand that customers deserve food that is carefully prepared and served in a clean environment.

We aim to maintain good standards of cleanliness in food preparation and dining areas. We also value fresh ingredients, proper food handling and careful preparation.

Customer feedback is important to us because it helps us identify areas for improvement and provide a better experience.

Our Future Goals

Our goal is to continue improving our meals, customer service and overall dining experience. We want to make it easier for customers to learn about our menu, contact our restaurant and enquire about orders.

As we grow, we aim to explore better ways of serving customers, introduce suitable meal options and strengthen our connection with the community. This website is one step towards making information about Rex Restaurant & Takeaway more accessible online.

We also hope to keep our prices fair, keep our kitchen clean and listen carefully to what our customers tell us, so that every visit is better than the last.
