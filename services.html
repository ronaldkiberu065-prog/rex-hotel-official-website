Our Services

Rex Restaurant & Takeaway offers several ways to enjoy our meals. Choose the service that suits you best, or contact us if you are not sure which one to pick.

Restaurant Dining

Enjoy your meals in a welcoming environment with friendly customer service.

Take Away

Order food to take away and enjoy at home, at work or wherever you prefer.

Food Delivery

Contact the restaurant to ask about food delivery options and availability.

Events & Functions

Contact us to discuss food arrangements for gatherings and special occasions.

How to Get Started

1. Choose

Look through our menu and prices and decide what you would like.

2. Order or Ask

Use the order form, or send an enquiry about delivery or events through our contact page.

3. Enjoy

Dine with us, collect your takeaway, or wait for your delivery if it is available in your area.

Service Questions

Is delivery available everywhere?

Delivery depends on your location and availability. Please contact the restaurant to confirm before ordering.

Can you cater for a group or event?

Yes, we can discuss food arrangements for gatherings. Choose "Event Catering" on the contact form and tell us the date and number of guests.
