About Our Restaurant

Our Restaurant

Rex Restaurant and Takeaway is located in Mukono, Uganda. We aim to provide delicious meals, refreshing drinks and friendly customer service in a welcoming environment.

Our Mission

To serve tasty, quality and affordable food while providing reliable service and a satisfying experience for every customer.

Our Vision

To become a trusted restaurant in Mukono, recognized for good food, excellent customer care and dependable takeaway services.

Our Values

Quality, cleanliness, customer satisfaction, teamwork, honesty and respect guide the way we serve our customers.
